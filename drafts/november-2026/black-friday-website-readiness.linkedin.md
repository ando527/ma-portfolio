Black Friday is less than three weeks away. The best time to check your website was a month ago, but the second best is this week.

If you run a Shopify or Webflow store, or a promo page with a Black Friday offer, here's what I'd do before the sale:

Open the sale page and checkout on a real phone with wifi off. Adobe's US figures put mobile at 57.5% of online sales on Cyber Monday last year, so plenty of your customers will see exactly that.

Place a test order with a discount code and a second payment method.

Pick a date about a week out and stop changing things after it. A change made on Thursday night has had no time to show you what it broke.

Put your last order dates on the site. Australia Post's last Parcel Post dates for metro Christmas delivery are mostly 21 and 22 December, and earlier for WA and the NT.

The good news from Shopify's own help centre is that most small stores don't need special traffic preparation. Its example of a sale with 50,000 visitors and 1,000 orders in 24 hours needs none.
