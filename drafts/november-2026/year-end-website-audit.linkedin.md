A .au domain that expires on 22 December can stay dead until late January.

That's not a scare story, it's auDA's own renewal rule. You get 30 days after expiry to renew, and during that time the website and email both stop working. After that, anyone can register the name.

Most businesses close for a few weeks over Christmas. If the renewal reminder goes to someone who left two years ago, nobody notices until January.

So I wrote down what I'd check on a live site in the next six weeks, before everyone switches off:

- Domain expiry dates, and who receives the reminder
- Whether form notifications still land in a real inbox, and pass SPF and DKIM
- Opening hours on the website and Google Business Profile (special hours cover up to 6 days; longer shutdowns should be marked temporarily closed)
- Who still has admin access, and whether 2FA is on
- Last year's copyright date and any out-of-date content
- Who is watching the site while you're away

The full audit is on my site: [link to /articles/year-end-website-audit/]

If you've got a site that's been live a while, it's probably worth an hour.
