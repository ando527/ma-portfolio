Google says there is no special optimisation needed to appear in its AI answers. That one line is worth reading before you pay anyone for "AI visibility".

More people now ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity a question instead of searching the old way. So I went through what's actually documented about getting a small business website included.

The short version:

- To show up in Google's AI features, a page needs to be indexed and eligible for a snippet. Ordinary SEO.
- OpenAI, Anthropic and Perplexity each run separate bots for search and for training. Check your robots.txt, and your host, so you haven't blocked the search ones by accident.
- Ahrefs tracked 1,885 pages that added schema and saw no meaningful lift in AI citations. Still worth doing for regular search.
- Ahrefs found about 97% of llms.txt files got no requests in May 2026. Google says no AI system uses it.
- A BrightLocal survey (a local SEO vendor, sample and country unconfirmed) says AI use for local business recommendations went from 6% to 45% in a year. It also says AI answers draw on reviews, directories, your website and social, so keep your listings consistent.
