## What I build in Shopify

- **Custom Liquid sections and templates** for the homepage, collections and product pages, so the layout follows the brand instead of the theme.
- **Product grids with some life in them.** On We Got The Chocolates, hovering a product tilts and lifts the card, and a panel beside the grid describes whichever product you're looking at.
- **Details people talk about.** The same store hides five icons around the page; find them all and a discount code appears. Listeners posted their finds on Instagram before placing their first orders.
- **More than a shop.** Membership application forms, podcast players and video heroes sit alongside the products, so a store can also be the home for a community.
- **Mobile first.** Most shoppers arrive on a phone, so every section is designed at phone width before desktop.

## How a project runs

1. **Discovery.** What you sell, who buys it, and what the store needs to do besides sell.
2. **Design.** Built around your brand and your products.
3. **Build.** Custom sections in Liquid that your team can rearrange in Shopify's theme editor.
4. **Launch.** Tested on phones, tablets and desktops, with products, collections and checkout working end to end.
