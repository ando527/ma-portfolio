## What I build in Webflow

- **Custom designs, not templates.** Each site starts from the brand. For VennCap Real Estate that meant building a new identity into every page; for Speedcubing Australia it meant turning a brand refresh into a design system of Webflow variables, so future changes happen in one place.
- **A CMS your team can run.** Sippy Tom's staff update seasonal menus themselves. Speedcubing Australia's volunteer committee manages records, competitions and team members without a developer.
- **Integrations and custom code.** Booking embeds such as ResDiary, maps, and live data from outside APIs. Speedcubing Australia's competition finder reads the World Cube Association's API directly, so nobody types competitions in by hand.
- **Search and accessibility, built in.** Written titles and descriptions for every page, structured data, a checked sitemap, keyboard-friendly controls, real form labels and support for reduced-motion settings.

## How a project runs

1. **Discovery and wireframes.** Who the site is for, what they need to find, and how the pages fit together.
2. **Design.** In your existing brand, or alongside a brand refresh.
3. **Build.** CMS structure first, then responsive layouts and interactions, tested from phone width up.
4. **Launch.** Titles, descriptions, redirects and structured data checked, so a rebuild keeps the rankings the old site earned.
5. **Handover.** Your team edits content in Webflow's Editor without touching the design.
6. **Maintainence.** Not every client of mine wants to get their hands dirty after launch - I offer upkeep and maintainence services to keep your site running smooth and staying up to date after launch.
